Attractions at Glance

AttractionLocationWhy we Go
Magellan’s CrossP. Burgos Street, Cebu City, Central Visayas, 6000, Philippines.Magellan's Cross is a historic landmark in the heart of Cebu City, commemorating the arrival of Portuguese explorer Ferdinand Magellan in 1521 and the introduction of Christianity to the Philippines (sheltered in a small chapel near the Basilica Minore del Santo Niño). The cross is housed in a protective tindalo wood casing to preserve the original structure, and the chapel features colorful ceiling frescoes depicting key events in Cebu's history
Basilica del Santo Niño26 Osmena Boulevard 6000 Cebu City Central VisayasThe church of the Holy Child (which was originally called the San Agustin Church) was founded by Fray Andrés de Urdaneta, O.S.A. on April 28, 1565, the day when the image of the Holy Child was found in a partially burned hut. After the rediscovery of the image on May 8, 1565, Miguel Lopez de Legazpi initiated the founding of the first Spanish settlement in the Philippines. The monastery of the Holy Name of Jesus was later constructed on the spot of the house where the image of the Santo Niño was found.
Fort San PedroA Pigafetta Street Cebu City, Central VisayasFort San Pedro (Spanish: Fuerte de San Pedro; Filipino: Moog ng San Pedro) is a military defense structure in Cebu, Philippines, built by the Spanish under the command of Miguel López de Legazpi, first governor of the Captaincy General of the Philippines. It is located in the area now called Plaza Independencia, in the pier area of the city.The original fort was made of wood and built after the arrival of Legazpi and his expedition.
Taoist Temple5th Street Cebu City, Central VisayasThe temple was built by Cebu's substantial Chinese Filipino community in 1972. At an elevation of 110 metres (360 ft) above sea level, the temple is a towering, multi-tiered, multi-hued attraction accessible by three separate winding routes.
Sirao Flower GardenSirao Peak Trail Cebu City, Central VisayasKnown as a colorful paradise just outside Cebu City, Sirao Garden offers visitors an enchanting escape among flourishing flower beds and picturesque landscapes. The garden features a variety of stunning blooms, including the vivid celosia flowers, set against a backdrop of rolling mountains and peaceful walking paths. It’s a favorite spot for photography enthusiasts and nature lovers who appreciate its multiple scenic viewpoints and tranquil atmosphere. The garden’s accessible price and well-maintained grounds make it an inviting destination for anyone looking to enjoy a refreshing day surrounded by natural beauty. Sirao Garden perfectly blends tropical charm with artistic design, making it an essential stop on any Cebu itinerary.
