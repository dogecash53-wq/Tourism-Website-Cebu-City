Introduction

Welcome to Cebu City, one of the Philippines' most exciting destinations. Known as the Queen City of the South, Cebu offers a unique combination of historical landmarks, cultural traditions, delicious local cuisine, and beautiful attractions. Whether you are interested in exploring centuries-old heritage sites, experiencing colorful festivals, or discovering the city's vibrant lifestyle, Cebu has something special waiting for you.

Why visit Cebu City?

Cebu City is the capital of Cebu Province and the center of commerce, education, and tourism in Central Visayas. It was founded in 1565 by Miguel López de Legazpi, which makes it the oldest Spanish settlement in the country.
