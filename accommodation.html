Hotels & Accommodation in Cebu

Find the right place to stay in Cebu, from modern city hotels and beachfront resorts to peaceful island retreats, family-friendly accommodation and affordable guesthouses.

Cebu offers a wide range of accommodation for visitors with different travel plans, budgets and preferences. Whether you are planning a short city break, a beach holiday or a longer journey around the province, choosing the right location can make your visit more enjoyable.

Where to Stay in Cebu

Cebu is a large province with several distinct travel areas. The best place to stay depends on what you want to experience. Cebu City is convenient for shopping, dining, business and historical attractions. Mactan Island offers beach resorts and easy airport access, while destinations such as Moalboal, Bantayan Island and Malapascua provide a more relaxed coastal atmosphere. Travellers planning to explore several parts of Cebu may find it convenient to stay in more than one location.

Hotels in Cebu City

Cebu City offers everything from affordable hotels and small guesthouses to international hotel brands and upscale accommodation. Areas around Cebu Business Park and IT Park are popular with visitors who want convenient access to restaurants, shopping centres and modern city facilities. Other neighbourhoods provide accommodation closer to historical landmarks, local markets and traditional city life. Cebu City is particularly suitable for business travellers, short stays and visitors who want to explore the province while enjoying urban conveniences.

Beach Resorts on Mactan Island

Mactan Island is one of Cebu’s best-known resort destinations, offering beachfront accommodation, swimming pools, restaurants and recreational activities. Many resorts are located in Lapu-Lapu City, particularly around Maribago, Punta Engaño and neighbouring coastal areas. Accommodation ranges from luxury resorts with extensive facilities to smaller hotels and more affordable properties. Mactan is a convenient choice for travellers who want a beach holiday within relatively easy reach of Mactan-Cebu International Airport.

Accommodation in Moalboal

Moalboal is popular with divers, snorkellers and travellers who enjoy coastal activities. Panagsama Beach is known for its diving centres, restaurants, guesthouses and accommodation close to the famous sardine run. White Beach, also known as Basdaku, offers a different atmosphere, with sandy beaches and accommodation options in the surrounding area. Moalboal has a mixture of budget-friendly stays, small resorts and comfortable seaside properties.

Staying on Bantayan Island

Bantayan Island is a popular destination for visitors seeking white-sand beaches and a slower pace of life. Santa Fe is the island’s main tourist accommodation area, offering beachfront resorts, guesthouses, small hotels and holiday rentals. Visitors can enjoy coastal scenery, local restaurants and a relaxed atmosphere while exploring the island. Bantayan is particularly appealing for couples, families and travellers planning a peaceful seaside break.

Hotels and Guesthouses on Malapascua Island

Malapascua is a small island known for its diving opportunities, particularly encounters with thresher sharks. Accommodation includes diving resorts, small hotels, beachfront properties and budget guesthouses. Many visitors choose accommodation near the island’s main beaches and diving centres, making it easy to arrange boat excursions and underwater activities. Malapascua is especially suitable for diving holidays and visitors who enjoy small-island life.

Budget Accommodation and Hostels

Travellers exploring Cebu on a limited budget can find hostels, guesthouses, simple hotels and affordable private rooms in many destinations. Cebu City offers a particularly broad selection of budget accommodation, while tourist areas such as Moalboal and Bantayan also have economical options. When comparing accommodation, consider the location, transport connections, included facilities and recent guest reviews. A well-located guesthouse can sometimes provide a more convenient experience than a larger hotel farther from the places you want to visit.

Family-Friendly Accommodation

Families visiting Cebu can choose from city hotels, serviced apartments and beach resorts offering facilities suitable for children. Depending on the property, amenities may include swimming pools, family rooms, restaurants, recreational areas and convenient access to nearby attractions. For longer stays, apartments or accommodation with kitchen facilities may provide additional flexibility. Families planning a beach holiday may find Mactan Island and Bantayan Island particularly appealing, depending on their preferred activities and travel arrangements.

Reminder for Choosing the Right Accommodation

Before booking a hotel or resort in Cebu, consider your priorities.

Location is especially important because travelling between different parts of the province can take considerable time.

Check transport connections, property facilities, recent reviews and cancellation conditions. For island destinations, also consider ferry schedules and possible weather-related disruptions.

Travellers who want to combine city sightseeing with beach activities may benefit from dividing their stay between Cebu City and a coastal destination.
