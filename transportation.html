Transportation and Travel Guide

Getting There

  • By air: Mactan-Cebu International Airport, about 45 minutes to 1 hour from downtown depending on traffic.
  • By sea: Cebu piers connect to Bohol, Leyte, Negros, and Manila.

Travel tips

  1. Avoid Rush Hours (7 to 9 AM, 5 to 7 PM).
  2. Keep small bills for fares.
  3. Check weather during typhoon season (June to November).
